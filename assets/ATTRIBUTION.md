`dino-tard.png`, `dino-vita.png`) are pixel-art character sprites by
**Arks** (@ScissorMarks):

- Dino Characters — https://arks.itch.io/dino-characters
- License: free to use with attribution (see the itch.io page)

avoid, ghost — character "tard") are from the extended **Dino Family**
animation pack by **DemChing**:

- https://demching.itch.io/dino-family
- License: free to use with attribution (see the itch.io page)
